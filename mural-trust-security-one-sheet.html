Trust posture

Mural organizes its public trust-and-security story around four pillars: security, compliance, reliability, and privacy. Together, they frame the platform as an enterprise-ready environment for teams that need strong controls without slowing down collaboration.

The emphasis is on layered protection, operational transparency, documented policies, and governance capabilities that help procurement, security, and IT stakeholders evaluate risk with confidence.

Security

Dedicated security controls to protect customer data at every layer, backed by a public security policy and vulnerability disclosure policy.

Compliance

Alignment with global, regional, and industry regulations to support customer compliance obligations.

Reliability

Infrastructure investment designed to support deployments that scale to thousands of members.

Privacy

Commitment to collaborator privacy rights, supported by formal privacy and data processing documentation.
