Member pricing

Enterprise plan

Mural's seat-based pricing model offers a predictable approach to procurement and provisioning user access.

For Enterprise plan customers, the Member pricing model offers four user types: Full Members, Limited Members, Guests, and Visitors. Each user type has access to the capabilities and features that best fit their needs.

This model lets companies assign users the appropriate level of access in accordance with their security and compliance standards, while allowing users to collaborate seamlessly with clients, customers, and consultants without compromising security.

Predictable and straightforward pricing — no catches, no surprises.

Mural is committed to offering flexible and transparent pricing with no catches and no surprises.
