Trust & Security

Trust, security, and control for enterprise collaboration.

Mural maintains rigorous security, reliability, compliance, and privacy measures, paired with enterprise-grade administrative controls designed to help organizations collaborate with confidence.

Trusted by enterprise teams

50%+

of the Fortune 100, along with customers in highly regulated industries including financial services, government, and healthcare.

Mural organizes its public trust-and-security story around four pillars: security, compliance, reliability, and privacy. Together, they frame the platform as an enterprise-ready environment for teams that need strong controls without slowing down collaboration.

The emphasis is on layered protection, operational transparency, documented policies, and governance capabilities that help procurement, security, and IT stakeholders evaluate risk with confidence.

Security

Dedicated security controls protect customer data at every layer, supported by a public security policy and vulnerability disclosure policy.

Compliance

Alignment with global, regional, and industry regulations helps support customer compliance obligations.

Reliability

Infrastructure investment is designed to support deployments that scale to thousands of members.

Privacy

Formal privacy and data processing documentation helps organizations evaluate how collaboration data is handled.
