Mural for Agile teams

Level up your team's agile ceremonies and ship faster.

Mural gives agile teams a visual canvas to run standups, sprint planning, retrospectives, and backlog refinement — whether your team is in the same room or spread across time zones.

How agile teams use Mural

Sprint planning

  • Import work items from Azure DevOps or Jira
  • Prioritize and assign stories visually
  • Build capacity plans with real-time input

Retrospectives

  • Use structured templates to surface feedback
  • Vote on priorities with built-in tools
  • Capture action items and track progress

Backlog refinement

  • Break down features into smaller work items
  • Estimate effort collaboratively
  • Keep requirements visible and accessible

Program boards

  • Visualize dependencies across teams
  • Align on shared milestones and timelines
  • Keep distributed teams in sync
