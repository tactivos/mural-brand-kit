Member pricing

Enterprise plan

Predictable and straightforward pricing — no catches, no surprises.

Mural's seat-based pricing model offers a predictable, identity-driven approach to procurement and provisioning user access. For Enterprise plan customers, the Member pricing model offers four user types, each with access to the capabilities and features that best fit their needs.

This model supports SCIM provisioning, SSO enforcement, and predictable IT administration at scale. Admins can assign users the appropriate level of access in accordance with their security and compliance standards.

Full Members are licensed users who create murals, facilitate sessions, manage rooms, and publish or administer content. Limited Members include broader team members who collaborate, comment, and vote.

Contact a Mural account representative for more information.

Security

Enable secure, authenticated access with enterprise-grade controls including SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and configurable permissions.

Scalability

Drive collaboration while scaling confidently, with unlimited Limited Members included.

Control

Easily manage user access and permissions in the company dashboard.

Predictability

Clear pricing with Full Members paid per license, while unlimited Limited Members are included in the platform fee.
